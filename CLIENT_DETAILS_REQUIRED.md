# What We Need to Launch Your Website (SecondInnings50)

Here is a step-by-step guide to gathering the technical details required to make your website live. I have kept this as simple as possible!

### 1. GoDaddy Access (Domain Name)
*Why we need it:* To connect your domain name to the hosting server.
*How to get it safely:*
1. Log in to your GoDaddy account.
2. Click on your profile name (top right) and go to **Account Settings** -> **Delegate Access**.
3. Click **Invite to Access**.
4. Enter my Name and Email address.
5. Select **Products & Domains** access level and click **Invite**.

### 2. Hosting Account Access (cPanel / Server)
*Why we need it:* To upload the website code and set up the database.
*How to get it:*
- Please provide the Username and Password for your hosting provider (e.g., Hostinger, GoDaddy Hosting, Bluehost).
- *If you haven't purchased hosting yet, please let me know and I can guide you.*

### 3. Razorpay Payment Gateway Details
*Why we need it:* To collect the ₹99 Identity Verification Contribution Fee on the platform.
*How to get it:*
1. Log in to your [Razorpay Dashboard](https://dashboard.razorpay.com/).
2. On the left menu, scroll down to **Settings** -> click **API Keys**.
3. Click **Generate Live Key**.
4. Copy the **Key Id** and **Key Secret** and save them in a secure note.
5. *(Optional but easier: You can also just go to **Settings -> Team -> Invite Member** and invite my email as an Admin, and I will copy the keys myself!)*

### 4. Email Sending Details (SMTP)
*Why we need it:* So your website can send automated "Welcome" emails and "Safety Report" alerts.
*How to get it:*
- We need a professional email (like admin@yourdomain.com). 
- If you give me the Hosting Access (Step 2), I can usually create this email and find the settings (SMTP Host, Port, Password) for you.

### 5. SMS / OTP Gateway Details
*Why we need it:* To send mobile OTPs for user verification.
*How to get it:*
- If you already have an account with an SMS provider (like Twilio, Gupshup, or SMS Horizon), please provide the **API Key / Account SID**.
- *If you haven't set this up yet, don't worry. We can set up a basic SMS provider account together.*

### 6. WhatsApp Group Links
*Why we need it:* The website automatically routes approved members to city-wise WhatsApp groups.
*What you need to do:*
1. Open WhatsApp and create groups for your main cities (e.g., Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, Bangalore, Pune, Hyderabad).
2. Go to the group info, click **Invite via Link**, and copy the link.
3. Paste these links into a document and send them to me.

### 7. Basic Website Content
- **Logo & Images:** High-quality logo and any specific photos you want on the homepage.
- **Legal/Contact Info:** Your official phone number, support email, and physical address for the Contact and Terms pages.

---
**💡 Quick Tip:** If the Razorpay or SMS steps sound too technical, please just complete **Step 1 (GoDaddy)**, **Step 2 (Hosting)**, and **Step 6 (WhatsApp Links)**. We can get on a quick 10-minute call and I will guide you through the rest via screen share!
